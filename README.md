## 🔥 What I'm Shipping

### Basis — [basis-peach-nu.vercel.app](https://basis-peach-nu.vercel.app)

Market intelligence you can audit. Live signals go through a deterministic rule engine — no language model anywhere in the decision path — and come back as one stance on crypto exposure with a receipt holding the evidence and the rule that produced it. Anyone can replay that receipt and check the conclusion without trusting me.

One engine serves all three hackathon tracks: a practice agent that journals every decision it would have made, the briefing that explains what changed and why it mattered, and two standalone skills that need no credential.

`14 ordered rules` · `488 tests` · `61,440 decision states verified` · `no model in the decision path`

The state sweep earned its keep: it caught a real defect where an unreadable RSI turned a Hold into an Add.

### Twinstate — [twinstate.vercel.app](https://twinstate.vercel.app)

A living model of your body built from what you take. One timeline for prescriptions, painkillers, energy drinks and sleep; organ-level scores recomputed daily; and every interaction flag opens its rule, its sources and the records behind it. Built on the Ontomorph/HOLON platform.

Educational, not diagnostic — it explains what is known about what you log and points to real care when something looks urgent.

### ScopeShield — [scopeshield-five.vercel.app](https://scopeshield-five.vercel.app)

A Flask SaaS that catches scope creep for freelancers and agencies before it costs them money. Client requests come back as In Scope, Ambiguous or Out of Scope, with a change-order generator and a dashboard tracking revenue protected.
