## Projects

### [Bizi](https://www.usebizi.app)

**A money assistant for small businesses, built for Zenith Bank's Zecathon 6.0.**

Owners import their bank statements, count their stock and log cash and credit sales, and Bizi tells them what they made, who paid and what they can safely spend.

- Product manager and ideator on a three-day team build
- Only confirmed money reaches the totals, and an ambiguous match stays a question for the owner instead of becoming a decision
- Ask Bizi answers spending questions in English or Pidgin, and every naira figure it gives must trace back to the business's own records

`Team build` · `3 days` · `60 Nigerian banks and fintechs recognised` · `Live`

[Live](https://www.usebizi.app)

### [Twinstate](https://github.com/Bel-enie/twinstate)

**A living model of your body, built from what you take. Award-winning at the 2026 Ontomorph AI Hackathon.**

Log prescriptions, painkillers, energy drinks and sleep, and Twinstate checks the combination for interactions and shows where it adds up on a 3D body.

- Five engineered prompts; the four that drive the interface must return one JSON object in a fixed schema, and every response is validated and clamped before it renders
- The model never produces a clinical score: the numbers come from a rule engine, and the AI may only raise a severity, never lower it
- Every flag opens its rule, its sources and the records behind it; urgent symptoms are routed to real care

`Solo` · `7 days` · `5 engineered prompts` · `41 tests`

[Live](https://twinstate.vercel.app) · [Source](https://github.com/Bel-enie/twinstate)

### [Basis](https://github.com/Bel-enie/Basis)

**Crypto market intelligence you can audit. Every call comes with a receipt.**

Live market signals go through a deterministic rule engine and come back as one stance, Add, Hold, Trim or Wait, with a receipt holding the evidence and the rule that fired. Anyone can replay the receipt and check the answer without trusting me.

- No language model anywhere in the decision path, which is what makes the replay meaningful
- One engine serves all three hackathon tracks: a practice agent, a briefing and two standalone skills
- An exhaustive sweep of every decision state caught a real defect: an unreadable input turned a Hold into an Add

`Solo` · `14 ordered rules` · `488 tests` · `61,440 decision states verified`

[Live](https://basis-peach-nu.vercel.app) · [Source](https://github.com/Bel-enie/Basis)
